About Kovai Aanandha Catering

Kovai Aanandha Catering is dedicated to serving authentic South Indian cuisine with fresh ingredients, hygienic preparation, and exceptional service. We specialize in weddings, corporate events, family functions, birthdays, and special occasions across Coimbatore. Our goal is to make every meal memorable with quality, taste, and professionalism — a true Arusuvai Aanubhavam.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often. Still curious? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do you provide catering for weddings?
Yes. Wedding catering is one of our core specialities — from traditional banana leaf feasts to live counters, sweets and grand buffet spreads, we design the full menu and service around your ceremony and reception.
How is your catering priced?
Pricing depends on your menu selection, guest count, service style (buffet, leaf service or live counters) and event location. Share your requirements on WhatsApp or call us and we'll send a custom quotation within minutes, free of charge.
What is the minimum guest count for booking?
We cater to intimate family gatherings as well as large weddings with hundreds of guests. Reach out with your expected headcount and we'll confirm whether it fits our current packages.
How do I book Kovai Aanandha Catering for my event?
Simply message us on WhatsApp or call +91 90879 99287 with your event date, guest count and preferred menu. We'll confirm availability, share a quotation and lock your date with a booking advance.
Do you provide outdoor and open-air catering?
Yes, we regularly cater outdoor functions, marriage halls, temple grounds and open-air venues across Coimbatore, complete with live counters and full on-site setup.
